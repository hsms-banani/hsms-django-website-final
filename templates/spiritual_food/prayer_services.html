Sunday Mass in Dhaka – Common Questions

Information for parishioners, visitors, and pilgrims attending Mass in Banani, Dhaka

When is Sunday Mass held in Dhaka at Holy Spirit Major Seminary?

Sunday Mass at Holy Spirit Major Seminary Chapel in Banani, Dhaka is celebrated with multiple service times throughout the day, including morning Mass at 6:30 AM and evening Mass services at 4:30 PM and 6:30 PM.

Where is Holy Spirit Major Seminary located in Dhaka?

The seminary is located at Block A-112, Road 27, Banani, Dhaka 1213, Bangladesh. It is easily accessible for residents and visitors across Dhaka.

Is Sunday Mass open to visitors and the general public?

Yes, all Catholic faithful, parishioners, students, and visitors are warmly welcomed to participate in Sunday Holy Mass, Eucharistic adoration, and daily devotions at our chapel.

Need Special Mass Intentions or Prayers in Dhaka?

Submit your intentions to our seminary community. We are honored to remember you, your family, and your intentions during Sunday Mass and daily prayers.
